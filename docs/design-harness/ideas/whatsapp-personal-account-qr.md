# WhatsApp connects via the human's personal account (QR bridge)

The interface signs in as the human's personal WhatsApp account via a QR bridge, not the official Cloud API business number. Supported by [Baileys](../sources/github/baileys.md) and [whatsapp-web.js](../sources/github/whatsapp-web-js.md). The human accepted the ban risk of the unofficial bridge on adjudication.
