## Post-fix simulator verification

Checked: 2026-09-07T19:23:47Z

After adding bounded removal retries, the rebuilt app reproduced the WebKit load and
then completed **Disconnect WhatsApp** automatically with:

- Disconnect state: `profile removed`
- Diagnostic: `Dedicated WebKit profile removed after 2 attempts...`
- No stale intermediate removal-failure diagnostic was shown

The simulator build succeeded. It still emitted six pre-existing trailing-closure
warnings in `SQLiteContextPersistence.swift`; none were related to this change.

This confirms that the simulator can exercise the off-screen `WKWebView`, dedicated
profile initialization, navigation, JavaScript evaluation, and browser-primitive
probes. It does not prove WhatsApp authentication, phone-number linking, linked-session
persistence, reconnect synchronization, or disconnect cleanup on a real iPhone.

## Decision boundary

Keep P0.3/P0.4 open until the physical-iPhone checks in `native-ios/README.md` are
completed with a burner account. Do not implement real bridge ingestion or close the
physical transport issues from this simulator-only result.
